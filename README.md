# 🏀 Who Are the NBA’s Most Efficient Shooters by Zone?

## 📌 Project Overview & Objective
Designed for basketball operations staff, coaches, and analytics teams, this project evaluates spatial shot distribution and shooting efficiency across 10 of the NBA's premier high-usage scorers. The goal is to translate zone-by-zone shooting splits into clear visual insights for scouting, defensive game-planning, and shot-profile optimization.

### Players Analyzed
Luka Dončić | Shai Gilgeous-Alexander | Anthony Edwards | Jaylen Brown | Tyrese Maxey | Kawhi Leonard | Donovan Mitchell | Nikola Jokić | Giannis Antetokounmpo | Joel Embiid

### Court Zones Evaluated
* **At Rim (0–3 Feet)**
* **Paint (Non-RA)**
* **Midrange**
* **Corner Three**
* **Above-the-Break Three**

## 💡 Key Analytical Takeaways
* **Takeaway 1:** Giannis directs 61.0% of his FGA to 0-3 feet--the most interior-heavy profile in the sample.
* **Takeaway 2:** Luka, Mitchell and Edwards each take more than 40% of their shots from three
* **Takeaway 3:** Elite scorers reach production through very difficult shot mixes; volume alone does not explain efficiency. 
* * **Takeaway 4 — Strategic Applications for Coaching & Front-Office Evaluation:**
  * **Defensive Game-Planning (Coaching Staff):** By cross-referencing zone attempt share with FG%, staffs can tailor defensive coverages—running elite Above-the-Break creators off the three-point line while conceding non-paint midrange pull-ups to scorers whose efficiency drops outside 16 feet.
  * **Lineup Spacing & Role Allocation (Player Development):** Knowing where primary stars generate their highest-volume touches allows coaches to slot secondary players into complementary zones (e.g., pairing a paint-dominant scorer like Giannis or SGA with high-efficiency Corner 3 spacers).
  * **Salary Cap & Acquisition ROI (Basketball Operations / Business Side):** Front offices can avoid paying a premium for raw points-per-game by evaluating *where* those points are generated—acquiring cost-effective players whose zone efficiency directly fills a roster's spatial deficit.
